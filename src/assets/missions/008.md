## Mission Title: Bad Seed

Greetings mercenaries. This request comes from **Oliver Conrad**, a representative of the farmers working on artificial colony **Asteria-03**. Please, pay attention to the following message.

*"Howdy Royals. Been a while since we last talked. I have requested that Atlas gives this one to you specifically, given how good you've been to us in the past. Things were going smoothly for a while, but unfortunately, we've ran into yet another problem. Since a few weeks ago, we've been receiving fewer and fewer agricultural supplies from one of our main vendors. With the war effort on both sides of Atlas re-igniting, we find ourselves unable to meet demand and both Hesperia and the LAC are pressuring us to deliver. All attempts to reach out to the vendor have failed, who claims that the supplies shipped each week are as agreed in our contract. They've promised to investigate but frankly, I cannot afford to wait that long, as they seem to be dragging their feet. I need you all to look into this and find out what's going on. Please, stop by *Asteria-03* so we can discuss this further."*

# Objectives
- Investigate why supplies are not being delivered to Asteria-03.
