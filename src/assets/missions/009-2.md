## Mission Title: Business as Usual

Greetings mercenaries. This request comes from **Henry Pinkerton**, owner of a private investigation agency. Please, pay attention to the following message.

*"Greetings mercenaries, my name is Henry Pinkerton, acting head of Pinkerton P.I, an investigation firm specializing in security contracts in the civilian and industrial level. We have been contracted by the factory owners of Efesto to disperse the union workers who have halted production of important modules, necessary to the war effort. As we speak, the halt of all production operations has been impacting the supply lines of the Hesperian-LAC border conflict in the Acree-04 region. This has led to the unnecessary loss of equipment and most importantly, Hesperian soldier lives. We suspect that the union is bankrolled by someone who wants to see this prolonged as much as possible, further weakening Hesperia's military standing and lining the pockets of major players in the Workers' Union, by encouraging the proposal of ridiculous demands to the factory owners. I will offer more information at the moment of contract acceptance."*

# Objectives
- Assist **Pinkerton P.I** with breaking the Workers' Union's strike.

# Bonus Objectives
- Bonus objectives will depend on the outcome of the actions taken by the group.

# Rewards

- Class VI advancement.
