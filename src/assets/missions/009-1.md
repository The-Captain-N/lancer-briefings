## Mission Title: A Union in Peril

Greetings mercenaries. This request comes from **George Williams**, president-elect of the independent Workers' Union situated in **Efesto**. Please, pay attention to the following message.

*"Name's Williams, George Williams. We, the Efesto Workers' Union seek support in our fight against the factory owners who seek to fund the system's war machine with our blood, sweat and tears. For a few weeks now, we have halted almost all production facilities across the planet in protest of a new regime of investors coming in and demanding sweeping changes to our operations, leading to more hours and lower pay, in order to appease Hesperia high command. We will not stand on our rights being impeached. Should we cave in now, capital owners across Hesperian territory, as well as independent colonies and the LAC will attempt to use us as an example to crush unions that seek to assert the value of the working man. We have been the target of multiple harassment operations from so called "neutral parties", but we ain't buying it. Help us repel the strike-breakers and teach a lesson to these pigs."*

# Objectives
- Assist Efesto's workers in repelling strike-breaking attempts.

- Bonus objectives will depend on the outcome of the negotiations.
